Non-Stick & Release Coatings

Our core business. Teflon™ and PTFE fluoropolymer non-stick coatings, applied to industrial and commercial parts that need easy release, quick cleanup and long service life.

What we coat

Bakeware and food-processing equipment, moulds and release surfaces, industrial paint grates and fixtures, rollers, chutes, hoppers and any part where product sticking costs you time.

Why it pays

From bakeware to industrial paint grates, our low-friction coatings enable quicker clean-up, reducing man-hours while increasing productivity. Less downtime, less scrap, less labour.

Chemical resistance

Halar® ECTFE: when chemical resistance is essential, Halar is the pin-hole-free coating you need for process equipment in Chemical Valley and beyond.
